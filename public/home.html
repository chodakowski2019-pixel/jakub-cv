What I'm building

Bruno AI

Your reps practice on an AI customer before they call a real one. He talks back out loud, and every word gets scored.

Live voice calls

Bruno plays your customer. He pushes back, asks for numbers and tries to end the call early.

3 levels

Easy, normal and tough. Reps start friendly and work up to the buyer who says no to everything.

Score after every call

A coach grades the call on one clear rubric and shows what to fix next time.

Daily practice

Mistakes turn into short drills. Bruno brings them back until the skill sticks.
